Drathir Vayshan
Dragonborn Paladin — Character Background
Origin: The Vayshan Clanhold

Drathir Vayshan was born under the banner of Clan Vayshan, a warrior lineage tracing its ancestry to an ancient brass dragon named Vaelithra. He was raised in a fortress-hold built into the cliffs above a mountain pass — a place where every child learns the weight of a blade before they learn to read. Dragonborn clans measure worth through service and honor, and Vayshan was no exception: Drathir's scales, a burnished copper-gold, marked him early as one destined for the temple-barracks rather than the forge or the fields.

The Defining Wound

When Drathir was young, the hold was betrayed from within — a rival clan, hungry for the pass's trade routes, bribed a gate-warden to open the way in the dead of night. He survived the massacre because his instructor, an aging paladin of the Order of the Unbroken Flame, shielded him with her own body and pressed her holy symbol into his hands as she died. The hold burned. Half his clan was put to the sword. Drathir was twelve.

The Oath

He spent the following years as little more than a beggar-warrior, drifting between mercenary work and thin survival, until grief calcified into purpose. Kneeling in the ashes of his instructor's shrine, Drathir swore an oath — whether of Devotion (to never again let cruelty go unanswered), Vengeance (to hunt down every hand that lit that fire), or Redemption (having done dark things himself in those hungry years, and needing to become someone worth forgiving) is yours to choose, and it will color everything about how you play him.

Who He Is Now

Drathir carries his instructor's holy symbol still, scarred and soot-stained, as his own. He speaks the Draconic tongue of his clan more easily than Common, and he never fully lost the hierarchical instincts of hold-life — he craves a chain of command, a cause bigger than himself, even as he chafes against unjust authority. Battle-oaths matter to him in a way that outsiders sometimes find rigid or old-fashioned; a promise made is a debt owed, full stop.

Ideal, Bond, Flaw
Ideal: "A hold is only as strong as the oaths that bind it. I will not be the crack that brings the next one down."
Bond: Clan Vayshan's survivors are scattered across the realm. Rebuilding what was lost — the hold, the name, the trust — drives every choice Drathir makes.
Flaw: He struggles to trust anyone who hasn't proven themselves in a crisis, and has a hard time forgiving betrayal, even small ones, because of what one betrayal already cost him.
